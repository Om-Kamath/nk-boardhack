The five questions every VC asks

Survive these five and you've survived most first meetings. The pitch generator gives each one a timed slot.

  1. “Show me the week someone paid you.” Traction isn't a logo slide — it's a week of real usage or revenue.
  2. “Why is this possible today that wasn't last year?” If nothing changed, the timing story is thin.
  3. “What do you own that a competitor can't clone by Friday?” Data, distribution, or a workflow — something that compounds.
  4. “Who exactly buys this, and how do they find out it exists?” “Developers” is not a buyer; “security teams at 500-person fintechs” is.
  5. “Why is this team the one that wins it?” Obsession, unfair advantage, or both — said in five seconds.
